[AI]: A benchmark is a standardized test used to compare the performance of different models. It provides a common measurement so progress can be tracked and results compared fairly.

[HUMAN]: What is generalization in machine learning?
[AI]: Generalization is the ability of a model to perform well on data it has not seen during training. It is the ultimate goal of machine learning and what distinguishes a useful model from one that merely memorizes.
